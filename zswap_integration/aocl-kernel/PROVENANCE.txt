Kernel-safe adaptation of lz4.c
-------------------------------
The compressor used is AOCL's scalar-optimized path
(AOCL_LZ4_compress_fast_extState_internal -> AOCL_LZ4_compress_generic ->
AOCL_LZ4_compress_generic_validated). The AVX and threaded paths are opt-in.
lz4.c is compiled under kernel-safe flags (freestanding, no SIMD/FPU, no
autovectorization) and exposed to the kernel module / benchmark driver through
glue.c. Decode optimizations are macro-gated and hint-only; with the macros
undefined the emitted/decoded byte stream is identical to upstream.
